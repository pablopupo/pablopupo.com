Posts on this site are MDX files in `content/posts`. Each one starts with a
small block of frontmatter, and a `draft: true` flag keeps a post out of the
writing page, the RSS feed, and the sitemap until it is ready.

```yaml
---
title: How this site publishes
date: "2026-07-02"
draft: true
---
```

Publishing is deleting that flag. This post exists to keep the pipeline
honest, and it ships with the flag still on.
