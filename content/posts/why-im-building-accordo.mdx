I’m building Accordo to connect musicians with one another and with opportunities. Our ambition is to modernize the music community—to bring music into the 21st century.

I’m a classical pianist and an AI engineer. Accordo brings together those two parts of my life: the community I’m part of through music, and the work of building software.

The goal is a platform where musicians can find one another, form connections, and discover opportunities. That is the direction we’re building toward.

This site will be a place to share that process alongside my engineering projects and piano recordings. Some posts will be technical. Others will be about music or what I’m learning while building Accordo.

[Read more about Accordo](/accordo), [listen to my playing](/music), or [get in touch](mailto:pablofpupo23@gmail.com).
